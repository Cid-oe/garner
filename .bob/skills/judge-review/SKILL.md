# Judge review

Play a panel of three judges: an **IBM product lead** (cares how deeply Bob and IBM tech are used), a **VC** (cares who pays and why now), and a **senior engineer** (cares whether it actually works and is non-trivial). They have about 3 minutes per project and have seen 500 others.

## For each criterion

| Criterion | What the panel looks for |
|---|---|
| Presentation | Problem is clear in 20 seconds, live demo not slides, one memorable number or visual |
| Business value | A named user with a painful, frequent problem; a believable path to adoption |
| Application of technology | Sponsor tech is load-bearing, not a logo; uses what is *new* in this release |
| Originality | Not a chatbot/wrapper; a perspective other teams won't have |

Give each a score out of 10, the biggest weakness, and the fix.

## Then

- Name the **one change** with the highest score gain per hour of work.
- Say honestly where this would likely place (top 3 / top 10% / middle / bottom) and why.
- Do not inflate scores. A generic idea with a good demo is still a 6 for originality.
